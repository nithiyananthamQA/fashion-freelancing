A simple marketplace for the people who make fashion.

Fashion shouldn't be hard to make. We built Fashion Freelancing because the freelance designers, pattern makers, and photographers we worked with kept getting lost on generic sites — buried under logo designers and SEO writers. So we made one just for fashion.

Why we built this.

Our founders worked at Versace, A.P.C. and Issey Miyake. They watched two things go wrong over and over:

  • 1 Brilliant freelance designers couldn't get found by the right brands. They were lost on Upwork next to people who didn't know what a tech pack is.
  • 2 Brands wasted half their budget on fashion agencies. They paid ten times more for the same work, just because they didn't know how to find a good freelancer themselves.

So we built Fashion Freelancing: the place where fashion brands and fashion freelancers find each other directly. No agencies. No middlemen.
