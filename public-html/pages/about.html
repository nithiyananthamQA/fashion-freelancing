Why we exist

Fashion runs on handoffs. Most of them leak.

A designer sketches. A tech pack translates. A factory interprets. A photographer reshoots. A copywriter describes. Every handoff between those people is where money leaks — a measurement misread, a print that breaks in repeat, a listing nobody finds.

We built one team that does the whole chain, so the handoffs happen inside the studio instead of across ten freelancers' inboxes. The tech pack writer knows what the pattern maker needs. The 3D artist works from the same spec the factory will. The listing writer has the garment's real measurements.

That's the entire idea. One brief in, one delivery out.

What we hold ourselves to

The rules we don't bend.

01

Fixed quotes, honoured

The number we quote is the number you pay. Scope changes get a new quote before work, never a surprise invoice after.

02

Two rounds, on purpose

Unlimited revisions reward vague briefs and endless projects. Two structured rounds force the conversation that matters.

03

You own everything

Designs, patterns, prints, source files — yours on final payment. We never resell or re-colour a client's work.

04

Factory language

EPI and PPI, DXF and AAMA, GSM and grade rules — we write specs the way production people read them.

05

Honest proof only

No fabricated testimonials, no borrowed brand logos, no invented numbers. If we can't show it honestly, we don't show it.

06

Confidential by default

Unreleased collections stay unreleased. Client work never appears publicly without written permission. NDA on request.
