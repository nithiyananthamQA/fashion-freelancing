How a project runs

Four moves. No surprises.

Every project is sized the same way — so you know when files arrive, when revisions happen, and when the last invoice clears.

01. Brief

You send a reference.

Sketch, sample, screenshot or a paragraph. We come back with scope, price and a delivery date inside 24 hours.

02. Setup

One channel, one lead.

A shared workspace is opened. One person owns your project end to end. NDAs signed before any reference moves.

03. Build

Drafts. Reviews. Revisions.

You see work as it's made - not at the end. Two revision rounds are baked into every package.

04. Handover

Files you can use.

Factory-ready PDFs, plot-ready DXFs, channel-ready listings, deployed sites. Plus a 14-day touch-up window.

FAQ

The usual questions.

If yours isn't here, drop it on the form below — replies within 24 hours.

How do you price a project?+
We scope each service against your brief. You always get a fixed price and a fixed date before any work starts - never an hourly meter. For multi-service projects, the bundle gets a friendlier number than buying each piece on its own.
Can I hire you for just one service?+
Yes. Every one of our 10 services can be booked stand-alone. Most brands start with one - a tech pack, a CLO3D sample, an AI shoot - and add more once the first delivery lands.
Do you sign NDAs?+
Always. NDAs are signed before any reference moves. We use a mutual NDA by default; happy to sign yours instead.
Who owns the files at the end?+
You do - fully and exclusively, on final payment. Working source files (PSD, AI, DXF, CLO project, repo) are part of the handover.
How fast can you turn a project around?+
Single-service work usually lands in 2–7 days from approval. Websites and AI agents run 2–6 weeks depending on scope. Tight deadline? Tell us on the brief - we'll say yes or no honestly.
Do you work outside India?+
Yes. We work with brands across the US, EU, UK, GCC, and India. Async by default; calls in your time zone when needed.
