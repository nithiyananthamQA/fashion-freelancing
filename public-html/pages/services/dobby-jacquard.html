Which loom does your idea need?

Dobby or jacquard — we'll tell you straight.

If the cheaper loom can do it, we say so. That's the point of the 24-hour quote.

Dobby

  • Geometric textures, stripes, checks, small motifs
  • Repeat limited by the shafts — typically up to 16–32 ends
  • Faster and cheaper to run — the workhorse of shirting
  • Pique, oxford, herringbone, waffle, seersucker effects

Jacquard

  • Every warp end controlled — pictorial designs, no repeat limit in the width of the harness
  • Damasks, brocades, tapestry, logos, portraits in cloth
  • Multiple weave structures inside one design — matt against satin, sheen against shadow
  • Designed to your machine's hook count, exactly
Colorway economics

Change the weft, not the warp.

At the mill, swapping weft yarns between colorways is quick and cheap. Re-beaming a new warp is neither. So we design your colorway range to pull as much variety as possible from weft changes alone — and when a colorway genuinely needs a new warp, we flag it before you commit, not after the mill invoices you.

This is the kind of decision that's invisible in a JPEG and very visible in your production cost.
