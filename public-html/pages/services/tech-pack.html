Buy this if

  • You are sending a style to two or more factories and want quotes you can actually compare.
  • Your last sample came back wrong and nobody can point to what it was measured against.
  • You are ordering 100–1,000 pieces, where a factory gives you the least hand-holding.
  • You want the option to move factories next season without redrawing everything.

Do not buy this if

  • You are reordering an approved style from a factory that already holds your approved spec.
  • You are buying ready stock or private-label blanks with only a logo change — you need a print spec, not a pack.
  • The design is not settled. Sort the silhouette first; a pack drawn around a moving target gets redrawn.
  • You want an hourly designer on call. We quote fixed, per pack, and that is the whole arrangement.
