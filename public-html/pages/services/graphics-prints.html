Repeat types, explained

You don't need to know which one you need. We do.

Tell us the garment — we pick the repeat that hides the mechanics and flatters the cut.

Full drop

Motifs align in a straight grid — clean, architectural, great for geometrics and stripes-with-motifs.

Half drop

Every second column shifts down half a tile — the eye stops finding the grid. The workhorse of florals.

Tossed

Motifs scattered in every direction — organic, dense, forgiving on any cut line. Kids, sleep, resort.
