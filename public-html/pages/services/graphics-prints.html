04 — Repeat types

The repeat decides where the join hides.

We pick it with you at the brief, because it decides how the motif sits once the cloth is cut.

Full drop

Motifs align in a straight grid — clean, architectural, and the easiest repeat for a mill to match across a roll.

Half drop

Every second column shifts down half a tile, so the eye stops finding the grid and the join hides in the motif.

Tossed

Motifs scattered in every direction — organic, dense, and forgiving on any cut, which is why swimwear lives here.
