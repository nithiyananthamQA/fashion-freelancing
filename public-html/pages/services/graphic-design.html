How consistency actually happens

We don't design assets. We design the system that designs them.

Asset-by-asset design

  • Every job starts from a blank canvas
  • Colours and fonts drift a little each time
  • Quality depends on who's available that week
  • The brand ages in patches

System-first design

  • Palette, type, spacing and image rules decided once, documented
  • Every asset — ours or anyone's — draws from the same source
  • Asset #40 matches asset #1
  • New platforms launch on-brand by default

The system document is part of the first delivery — and it's yours.

The revision policy

Two rounds. On purpose.

"Unlimited revisions" sounds generous and works terribly — it rewards vague briefs, invites committee feedback, and projects limp to a version nobody loves. Two structured rounds force the conversation that matters: what exactly should change, and why. Most work here is approved in round one because the brief was interrogated before pixels moved.

If the scope genuinely changes mid-project, we say so and re-quote honestly — we don't bury it in "revisions."
