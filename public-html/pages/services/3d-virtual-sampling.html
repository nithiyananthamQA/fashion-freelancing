Accuracy

Real fabric physics, not pretty pictures.

We simulate from your actual fabric spec — GSM, stretch percentage, drape — so the render behaves like the textile, not like a video game. Most sample changes come from fit and fabric issues; that's exactly what a 3D pass catches before you cut.

And honestly: a 3D sample is a design tool, not a replacement for your final checks. Most brands still make one physical proto before bulk — that decision stays entirely with you. If a construction is hard to simulate reliably, we'll say so up front.

Questions

Asked before every sample.

A tech pack is ideal, but a clear sketch with measurements and a fabric reference is enough to begin. The more spec you share — GSM, stretch, trims — the more accurate the sample.

We simulate from your actual fabric spec — weight, stretch percentage, drape — so the render behaves like the textile, not like a video game. Most fit and fabric issues show up here, before anything is cut.

Your first render lands within 24 hours of the brief. A full sample — renders, colorways, turntable and deck — typically delivers in 3–5 working days.

One revision round is included with every sample — fit, colorway, fabric or camera angle, adjusted in hours rather than another multi-week round.

CLO3D and Browzwear — the two industry-standard engines — chosen per garment type. You don't need a licence or any 3D skills; we run everything in-house.

Your factory still works from your tech pack, as always — the 3D sample removes the ambiguity around it. Renders and the turntable video answer construction and fit questions before anything is cut. Whether you also make a physical proto before bulk is your decision — many brands do one.

High-resolution renders, an MP4 turntable video and a formatted PDF deck — all produced in CLO3D or Browzwear, the industry-standard 3D software. Other formats can be discussed per project.
