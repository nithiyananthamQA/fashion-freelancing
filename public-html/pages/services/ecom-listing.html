02 — Why it matters

A great product with a weak listing is invisible inventory.

A shopper meets your listing three times: in search, on the page, and when the parcel arrives. It can lose them at each one.

  1. 01 · Search

    Nobody finds it

    Shoppers search “cotton kurta set women office wear” — a title that says “Beautiful Dress — New Collection” matches none of it.

    The fix A keyword-researched title and every attribute filled, so search and filters both find it.

  2. 02 · Product page

    Finders don't buy

    Copy that lists facts (“100% cotton”) without selling the outcome (“stays crisp through a 10-hour day”) loses the click it earned.

    The fix Bullets that answer the question a shopper was about to ask — fit, feel, care.

  3. 03 · The parcel

    Buyers return it

    Fashion's returns problem is largely a fit-information problem. A listing without honest size guidance pays for it twice — shipping both ways.

    The fix Size and fit content in words, matched to your real size chart.
