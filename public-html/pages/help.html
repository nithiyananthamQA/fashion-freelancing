If you're hiring someone

How do I find a good freelancer?

Search for what you need (e.g. "tech pack designer") or browse by category. Look at their work, ratings, and how fast they reply. Top-rated and Verified ones are usually a safe bet.

How do I pay?

Pay with card, bank transfer, or local payment. Your money goes to us first — we hold it safely. Once you approve the work, we send it to the freelancer.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

Ask the freelancer to fix it (most packages include free changes). If you still aren't happy, tell us — we step in within 48 hours and decide what's fair.

Can I cancel an order?

Before they start, yes — full refund. After they start, it depends on what they've already done. Our team helps decide.

If you're a freelancer

How do I get on the site?

Sign up free, fill in your page (10 minutes), wait for our team to check it (about 2 days). Then you're live.

How does Pro work? Do I need it?

You don't need it. Free works fine for most people. Pro ($24/month) helps if you want unlimited samples on your page, AI tools to write pitches, or a "Verified" badge that helps you stand out.

How do I get paid?

To your bank account or PayPal, in your local currency. Usually 1 working day after the buyer approves your work.

Do you take a cut?

No. You keep 100% of what you earn from each job. We only earn from the optional Pro membership.

Payments & safety

Where is my money held?

With Stripe, the same payment company most online stores use. It's locked there until the work is done.

What happens if there's a problem?

Click "Get help" on the order page. Our team looks at the messages and files, decides who's right, and either refunds or releases the money. Usually within 48 hours.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The price you see on a freelancer's package is exactly what you pay.

My account

I forgot my password.

Click "Forgot?" on the sign-in page. We'll email you a reset link.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, anytime. Settings → Privacy → Delete account.

How do I change my email?

Settings → Account → change email. We'll send a confirmation to the new address.
