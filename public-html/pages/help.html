The essentials

Asked before every project.

Send your brief through the form or by email. Within 24 hours (business days) you get a fixed written quote: scope, deliverables, price and delivery date. That number doesn't move unless the scope does — and scope changes are always quoted before we do the work.

Whatever you have. A sketch, a reference photo, a Pinterest board, a tech pack, a live store link — each service page lists what's ideal, but we'll tell you within 24 hours if anything essential is missing. You never need to arrive "ready".

A revision round is a consolidated set of changes to the delivered work within the quoted scope — most projects are approved in round one. A completely new concept, garment or direction is a new brief, quoted honestly in advance. Extra rounds beyond the two are available and priced before we start them.

You do — fully, on final payment. Designs, patterns, prints, files and source files where quoted. Nothing is resold, re-coloured for someone else, or shown publicly without your written permission. The details are in our Terms.

Confidentiality is the default — briefs, tech packs and unreleased designs are seen only by the people working on your project. A mutual NDA is available on request and signed before any sensitive file moves.

Terms are stated in each quote — typically an advance to start, with the balance due on delivery before source files and ownership transfer. Taxes (including GST where applicable) are additional where the law requires.

That's the point of the studio. Tech pack → 3D sample → pattern → listing is one connected chain here, not four vendors. Pick "a bit of everything" in the form and describe the end goal — we'll propose the path in the quote.

We work remotely with brands, boutiques, manufacturers and mills — wherever you are. Briefs, reviews and delivery all happen online, in your timezone's working hours as far as scheduling allows.
