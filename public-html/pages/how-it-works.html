How it works.

Three simple steps. The same idea, whether you want to hire someone or get hired.

1

Find someone good.

Search for what you need — for example: "tech pack designer" or "fashion photographer in Milan". Or browse our list of freelancers by category.

Every freelancer is checked by a real fashion person before they're allowed on the site. So you don't waste time on people who don't know what a tech pack is.

Find a freelancer →
2

Agree on a price.

Two ways to do this:

  • Pick a price they've already set.
    Most freelancers list a few packages — like "Tech pack for one piece, $120" or "Photography day, $1,200". Click, agree, done.
  • Send a custom job.
    Got a bigger project? Describe what you need. They'll reply with a price and a timeline. You agree, they start.
3

Pay only when you're happy.

Your payment goes to us first, not the freelancer. We hold it safely.

When the freelancer delivers the work, you check it. If you're happy, you click "I'm happy with this" and we send the money to them. If not, you ask for changes (or get a refund — our team helps).

That's it. No invoices, no chasing payments, no surprises.
