Common questions.

Do you take a cut of what freelancers earn?

No. Freelancers keep 100% of what they earn. We only charge an optional Pro membership ($24/month).

What payment methods can I use?

Card, bank transfer, and major regional payment methods. We use Stripe behind the scenes — same as most online stores.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes. 14 days, full access, no card needed.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You stay Pro until the end of the month you paid for, then you're back on free.

What if a project goes wrong?

Our team helps. Within 48 hours, we decide who gets the money — usually a refund or a redo.
