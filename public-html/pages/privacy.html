Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 October 2026 · Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy applies to fashionfreelancing.com, its sign-in and specialist-network features, and the services we provide through them. It explains what information we receive, including information from Google when you choose Google Sign-In, how we use and protect it, and how you can make a privacy request. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Who We Are

Fashion Freelancing operates fashionfreelancing.com and the related specialist network (together, the "Services"). For privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@fashionfreelancing.com. We handle the information described here to provide and secure the Services and to respond to people who contact us.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information you submit. Depending on the feature, this can include your name, email address, password (stored as a one-way password hash), country and time zone; company or specialist profile details; application answers and portfolio material; project briefs, hire requests, messages, saved specialists, uploaded files, and information you include in a quote enquiry or email. Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the work.
  • Google account information. If you choose Google Sign-In or connect Google to an existing account, Google sends us your Google account's stable identifier, verified email address, and name. We use these fields to identify the Google account, create or link your Fashion Freelancing account, and sign you in. We do not ask for access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendars, or other Google services.
  • Sign-in and security records. We create account and sign-in records such as account ID, sign-in method, verification status, timestamps, hashed session token, session expiry, and a limited user-agent string. Security controls may process your IP address to rate-limit abusive requests; rate-limit identifiers are stored as hashes where applicable.
  • Device and usage information. Our infrastructure may receive request metadata such as IP address, browser and device information, requested URL, referring page, and request time to deliver, diagnose, and protect the Services. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate visit statistics without setting an analytics cookie. We do not use precise location data.
  • Communications and service records. We retain correspondence, support requests, project and account activity, and messages needed to respond, operate the network, and keep appropriate business records.

3. Google Sign-In Data

Google Sign-In is optional. When you select it, your browser is sent to Google's account chooser. We request only the OpenID Connect scopes openid, email, and profile. Google returns a one-time authorization code to our server; our server exchanges it with Google and checks the identity and verified-email claims before creating or linking an account. We use the Google account identifier, email, and name only for authentication, account linking, account security notices, and ordinary account operation.

We store the Google account identifier alongside your Fashion Freelancing account, and store the email and name as account details. We do not store Google's access or refresh tokens, read Google content, or use Google account information for advertising, sale, profiling, or unrelated purposes. A short-lived, HttpOnly cookie holds temporary sign-in security values for up to 10 minutes and is removed when the callback is handled. Google processes the sign-in request under its own privacy terms; see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create, authenticate, link, and secure accounts, including completing a sign-in you start with Google;
  • Respond to enquiries, provide quotes, run the specialist directory, connect clients and specialists, and manage applications, projects, messages, and files;
  • Send service, account, and security messages, including notices when Google Sign-In is connected or removed;
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, prevent abuse of, and improve the Services;
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, and record-keeping obligations, and establish or defend legal claims.

5. Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We share only what is needed for the purposes above:

  • Google handles the account-chooser and identity-verification flow when you choose Google Sign-In. We send Google the authorization request and receive the identity claims described above.
  • Cloudflare provides the Services' hosting, database (D1), private file storage (R2), email delivery, and aggregate web analytics. Information is processed by the relevant Cloudflare service to operate the features you use.
  • People involved in your work. Information you submit for a project or specialist-network interaction is shown to the relevant Fashion Freelancing team members and, where the feature requires it, the client or specialist you contact. We do not make private project files public by default.
  • Legal and business events. We may disclose information when required by law or valid legal process, to protect people or the Services, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for their own advertising. Google and Cloudflare also process information under their own service terms and privacy notices.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use functional browser storage, not advertising cookies. The ff_sid HttpOnly cookie keeps a signed-in session and expires after 30 days. The ff_google HttpOnly cookie temporarily protects the Google sign-in round trip and expires within 10 minutes. The ff_shortlist cookie can remember specialists saved before sign-in, and ff_draft_* cookies can preserve an unfinished form while you sign in; these expire after 30 days and 30 minutes respectively. The ff-theme preference is stored in your browser's local storage and is not sent to us. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no analytics cookie and reports aggregate visits. Blocking or clearing functional cookies may prevent sign-in or lose a saved draft or shortlist.

7. Retention & Deletion

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, to provide the Services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. Account and profile information is retained while the account or related service remains active. Project, application, message, and business records may be retained for the duration of the work and for a reasonable period afterward for support, security, and legal record-keeping. Required legal and financial records are kept for the period required by law.

Google sign-in security state expires within 10 minutes. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days; expired session and security records are routinely pruned. If you request account or Google-data deletion, contact hello@fashionfreelancing.com. We will verify the request and delete or de-identify information we no longer need, subject to information we must retain by law, for legitimate dispute resolution, or in limited system backups and security records until their normal expiry. Closing an account also ends its active sign-in sessions. Disconnecting Google stops future Google sign-in for that account; the Google identifier is removed when the connection is removed.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for data in transit, restrict access to account and project information by role and authorization, store passwords as one-way hashes, store session identifiers as hashes, and keep project uploads in private object storage that checks authorization when a file is accessed. Google sign-in uses a short-lived authorization code, state and nonce checks, and PKCE. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach requires notification under applicable law, we will notify affected people and authorities as required.

9. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

You can choose not to use Google Sign-In, use the account's available password sign-in method, clear functional cookies in your browser, and request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also ask us to disconnect Google from your account. If Google is your only sign-in method, contact us and we will help with the request without asking you to provide your Google password.

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you additional rights, such as data portability, objection, restriction, or the right to complain to a data-protection authority. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it; this does not affect processing that already took place. To make a request, email hello@fashionfreelancing.com from the address associated with your account when possible. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

10. International Transfers

Fashion Freelancing serves people in multiple countries, and Google and Cloudflare may process information in countries where they or their infrastructure operate. Those countries may have privacy laws different from yours. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we use an appropriate transfer mechanism through our service providers.

11. Children

The Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to review and remove it where required.

12. Changes & Contact

We may update this policy as the Services or applicable requirements change. We will post the current version here and update the date above; where appropriate, we will also notify account holders of a material change. Questions, access or deletion requests, and Google Sign-In disconnection requests: hello@fashionfreelancing.com.
