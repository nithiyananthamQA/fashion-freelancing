Legal

Privacy policy.

Last updated 8 May 2026. We follow GDPR, CCPA and the spirit of treating you like a human.

What we collect

  • Account data — name, email, password (hashed), profile details you choose to share.
  • Portfolio & project data — images, files, briefs, messages and metadata you upload.
  • Payment data — handled by Stripe; we store transaction IDs, not card numbers.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, features used, device & browser, IP address (anonymized after 30 days).

Why we use it

  • Run the Service: matching, messaging, search, payments.
  • Improve the product (privacy-first analytics — no third-party tracking ad networks).
  • Send transactional emails. Marketing only with your opt-in.
  • Fraud detection & trust & safety.

Who we share with

Only the third parties needed to run Fashion Freelancing: Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Cloudflare (CDN/security), Sentry (error monitoring), PostHog (privacy-respecting analytics). We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can access, export or delete your data at any time from Settings → Privacy. EU/UK users have additional rights under GDPR; California users under CCPA.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active. After deletion, project history is retained for 90 days for tax/dispute reasons, then anonymized.

Security

Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). 2FA available. SOC 2 Type II in progress.
