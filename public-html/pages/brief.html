About the job

We're an indie fashion brand based in Paris. We make ready-to-wear womenswear, sold online and in 12 stores across Europe. Our spring 2027 collection is 10 pieces, and we need a designer to lead it.

What we want: Quiet, considered fashion. Hand-finished tailoring with a few simple silk pieces. No logos, no loud branding. Think Khaite, Lemaire, early Phoebe Philo Céline.

What we make from: Organic linen blend, wool crepe, hand-woven cotton. We already have suppliers in Lisbon and Como.

What you'll deliver

  • Mood board and concept (week 1–2)
  • Sketches and finalize shapes (week 3–4)
  • Tech packs ready to send to factory (week 5–8)

What we'll give you

  • Our brand book and visual archive
  • Direct line to our team and suppliers
  • Daily Slack chat with our founder and production lead

What we're looking for

Senior designer (5+ years) Done capsules before Knows tailoring CLO3D nice to have
