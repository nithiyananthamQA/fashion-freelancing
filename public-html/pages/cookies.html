Cookie policy.

We use cookies sparingly — only the ones the site needs to work. No advertising cookies, no tracking across other sites.

Cookie Purpose Duration
ff_sidKeeps you signed in. Only set when you sign in.30 days
ff_draft_*Holds a form you are part-way through — for example a project brief — while you sign in, so nothing you typed is lost.30 minutes
ff_shortlistRemembers the specialists you saved before signing in.30 days

Not cookies, but worth knowing

Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage (ff-theme) and never leaves your device. Visit counts come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not identify you.

Managing cookies

All of these are essential to the feature they serve, so there is nothing to switch on or off. You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time — blocking ff_sid will sign you out. Questions: hello@fashionfreelancing.com.
