What you get

A tech pack is the file your factory needs to make a garment. It has a sketch, all the measurements, the materials, and how everything is sewn together. If you don't have one, your factory can't make your design.

For each piece, I send you:

  • ✓Sketch from front, back & side
  • ✓Full materials list (with where to source)
  • ✓All measurements your factory needs
  • ✓Construction details (stitching, finishes)
  • ✓Editable PDF + working files

How I work: Send me your sketch or photo. I'll come back within an hour with a couple of questions. You answer, I work, you get the file in 24 hours.

Frequently asked

What file format do I get?

An editable PDF you can send to any factory, plus the working file (Adobe Illustrator) so you can change things later.

Will my factory accept this?

Yes. I've worked with factories in 14 countries. If your factory comes back with questions, I'll help sort them out for free.

Can I make changes after delivery?

Yes. The basic package includes 2 free changes. The bigger packages have more.

Do you sign a non-disclosure agreement?

Yes, free of charge. I sign before any work starts.
