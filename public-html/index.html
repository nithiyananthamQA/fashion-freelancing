Good to know

Questions you might have.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Browsing is free. If you're hiring, you pay only when you hire someone. If you're a freelancer, joining is free forever — and you keep 100% of what you earn. We have an optional Pro plan ($24/month) with extra features for serious freelancers.

How is this different from Upwork or Fiverr?

Those sites cover everything from logos to writing. We only do fashion. Every freelancer here is checked by a real fashion person before they're allowed to sell. So you're not searching through 10,000 logo designers to find someone who knows tech packs.

What if the work isn't good?

You don't pay until you say it's good. If there's a problem, our team steps in within 48 hours and decides what to do — usually a refund or a redo.

How do I get paid as a freelancer?

We pay you to your bank account or PayPal in your local currency. Usually within 1 working day after the buyer approves your work.

Can I message someone before I hire them?

Yes. Click their profile, hit "Message" — it's free.
